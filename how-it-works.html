HOW LCN WORKS

LCN is the trading platform for the biggest companies in the GTA universe. Buy stocks, invest in the most famous brands of Liberty City, Los Santos and Vice City, and sell whenever you want, all with SOL from your own wallet. Every stock lives on Solana, so ownership and every trade are public and verifiable on-chain.

FAQ

What is LCN?

A platform to buy and invest in the stocks of the biggest companies from the GTA universe. The companies come from the game, the SOL you trade with is real.

Which companies can I invest in?

The biggest brands of the GTA universe, created and listed by LCN. New companies show up on the market page as they go public.

Are my shares really on-chain?

Yes. Each stock is a Solana token: your shares sit in your wallet and every trade can be checked on Solscan.

How is the price set?

By supply and demand: when players buy a company, its price goes up; when they sell, it goes down.

Which wallet do I need?

Phantom. Connect it at the top of the page before trading.

Are there fees?

Solana network fees apply to every trade.
